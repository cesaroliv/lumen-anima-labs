Policy

Privacy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Lumen Anima Labs builds products with data minimization and user control in mind. This page describes the current pre-launch website and the intended privacy boundaries of Marketplace Sync Guard. It does not claim that internet use or third-party services can provide absolute anonymity.

Institutional website

This website does not need to collect personal information for ordinary browsing. This version has no forms, account system, analytics, advertising trackers or marketing cookies. GitHub Pages, the hosting and delivery provider, may process technical request information according to its own terms and policies.

Marketplace Sync Guard

Marketplace Sync Guard is designed to process operational files locally on the user's device. CSV and XLSX files, SKUs, product prices, stock levels and audit results are not sent to Lumen Anima Labs for auditing.

The product is read-only: it does not automatically change inventory, prices or listings. Local history, mappings and settings remain on the device unless the user explicitly exports them.

Pro licensing and payments

A future production release may contact a Lumen Anima Labs licensing service to validate Pro access. That service is intended to receive only the minimum information needed for entitlement, such as a signed license token and subscription identifiers. Operational files and seller data are outside the licensing payload.

Payments will be processed by an external payment provider. The provider will process payment and account information under its own privacy terms. Final provider details and production practices will be published before Live sales begin.

Data sharing and retention

This pre-launch website does not sell personal information or build advertising profiles. Because it does not collect form submissions, analytics or user accounts, Lumen Anima Labs does not maintain a website visitor database in this version.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Marketplace Sync Guard enters production, licensing becomes operational or the website gains new functionality. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new revision date.

Questions

Current public project and publisher information is available through the Lumen Anima Labs GitHub presence. Formal support and privacy contact details will be published before Live sales.
