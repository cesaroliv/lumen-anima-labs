What it catches

Small feed defects become expensive after launch.

The Doctor is deliberately narrow: catch structural feed problems, basic platform-readiness issues and cross-source drift before they turn into rejected items, broken destinations or campaign debugging.

01Required fields

Missing columns and empty values for the fields expected by the selected target.

02Identity

Duplicate product IDs and inconsistent presence between two catalog sources.

03Destinations

Invalid HTTP/HTTPS product and image links before traffic is sent into them.

04Commercial values

Availability, regular price and sale-price formats that can break downstream ingestion.

05Catalog drift

Price, availability, quantity and title differences between two exports keyed by ID/SKU.

06Portable evidence

Download findings as CSV so the diagnosis can become a repair checklist instead of disappearing in a browser tab.
