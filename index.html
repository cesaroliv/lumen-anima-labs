07 · Before you buy

No fine print.

What exactly do I receive?

The Obsidian Regalia digital collection with 30 high-resolution PNG artworks, organized into download packages, plus the commercial license and README.

Is this a physical product?

No. You receive digital files. Frames, environments, furniture and objects shown in the mockups are examples of possible applications only.

Can the artworks be printed at different sizes?

Yes. The masters are prepared at 16,000 px tall while preserving each composition’s original proportions. Final print quality also depends on the chosen size, material, printer and print process.

Can I sell products using the artworks?

Yes, within the commercial license: finished physical products and final client projects are allowed. Reselling or redistributing the source files is not.

When do I receive the download?

After Mercado Pago confirms the payment as approved, the delivery page releases the protected download packages.

Do the artworks use AI?

Part of the collection uses AI-assisted tools within the creative process, followed by curation, technical preparation, organization and review by Lumen.

What if I have a download problem?

Support is available at lumenanima.com/support.html. Include the order or payment identifier so we can locate the purchase.
