Principles

How we decide what to build.

  1. 01

    Useful before flashy

    The product should make real work clearer or easier before it tries to impress.

  2. 02

    Privacy by default

    Collect less, explain what is needed and keep sensitive data close to the user.

  3. 03

    Local-first when possible

    Not every task needs a server, an account or a permanent connection.

  4. 04

    No artificial lock-in

    Core utility should stand on its own, and user-created data should remain portable.

  5. 05

    Measurable problems

    A product must solve a problem users can recognize and outcomes they can assess.
